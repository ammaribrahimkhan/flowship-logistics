Who We Are

FlowShip Logistics Ltd was born on the road. Our founder drove a truck for a decade before starting this company, and that experience shaped everything — our rates, our support, our respect for drivers. We know what carriers actually need because we've been there.

Our Story

Dispatch, done the way carriers actually want it.

Our founder spent 10 years behind the wheel of an 18-wheeler before sitting down in 2010 and building the transport company he always wished existed. No runaround. No hidden fees. No dispatcher who didn't understand what it meant to be stuck in a weigh station at 2am with a bad load confirmation.

FlowShip Logistics Ltd launched out of a small office in Dallas with two dispatchers and a handful of carriers who trusted us with their livelihoods. We repaid that trust by working harder than everyone else — picking up the phone at 3am, negotiating rates that actually made sense, and fighting for our carriers on every load.

Today, we're a full-service logistics operation supporting hundreds of carriers and shippers across all 48 contiguous states. But we still answer every call personally. That never changes.

Our Mission

To empower truck drivers and carriers by providing reliable dispatch solutions that maximize profits while delivering exceptional service through honesty, transparency, and professionalism.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted dispatch companies in the United States by creating long-lasting carrier partnerships and delivering consistent value every single day.
